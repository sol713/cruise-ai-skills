# Shore Excursion Option Comparison Framework

Use this framework to compare official, independent, and self-guided port-day plans.

## Official Ship-Sponsored Excursion

Best for:

- Tight or complex port windows.
- Tender ports, ferry routes, long-distance attractions, or border crossings.
- First-time cruisers, families, mobility needs, or low risk tolerance.
- Expensive once-in-a-lifetime activities where missing the ship would be catastrophic.

Tradeoffs:

- Often costs more.
- Larger groups and less flexibility.
- Activity quality can be uneven by vendor.
- Schedule is safer but less customizable.

## Independent Provider

Best for:

- Better value, smaller groups, or specialized experiences.
- Comfortable port windows.
- Providers with clear cruise-passenger pickup and return terms.
- Activities that end well before the minimum return buffer.

Tradeoffs:

- The traveler owns timing risk.
- Cancellation and refund terms vary.
- Pickup points can be confusing near busy ports.
- Live availability and local conditions matter.

## Self-Guided Plan

Best for:

- Walkable ports, nearby beaches, simple museums, food walks, or city wandering.
- Travelers who want flexibility and can abandon the plan early.
- Budgets where paid tours are not necessary.

Tradeoffs:

- Requires local navigation and transport confidence.
- Weather, traffic, taxi availability, and safety judgment matter.
- No provider is managing timing for the traveler.
- Not suitable for complex transfers or low-risk groups unless the activity is close.

## Recommendation Priority

Choose the option that preserves the best combination of:

1. Safe return buffer.
2. Activity fit.
3. Traveler group fit.
4. Logistics simplicity.
5. Budget fit.
6. Flexibility if weather, illness, or ship timing changes.

Savings never override return-to-ship safety.
